# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React + Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel; aviantrails.in to be pointed at it when ready.
Chosen by the user (same stack as their Hollydaff site). Tours, lodges, trip reports and reviews live in typed data
files so they can be edited without touching layout code.

## Users

- **Serious birders and bird photographers**, mostly Indian urban professionals and retirees (Mumbai, Bengaluru,
  Pune, Delhi NCR, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad…), many of them repeat travellers with long life lists and big lenses.
  They are choosing *which* trip to spend a week and a lakh-plus on, and *who* to trust to put them in front of the
  target species in good light.
- **International photography groups** (e.g. a Taiwanese group in Bhutan 2026; earlier guests from Singapore and
  Thailand) looking for a reliable leader in the Himalaya and North-East India.
- **Nature travellers** wanting a birding stay in the Kumaon foothills (Kaladhungi/Corbett) or the Almora hills
  (Manila), sometimes with astro, waterfall or landscape photography.
- Their job on this site: decide whether Avian Trails is worth trusting, find a departure or design a custom trip,
  check dates and seats, and start a WhatsApp conversation with Rajesh.

## Product Purpose

Avian Trails (Nainital district, Uttarakhand) runs birding, bird-photography and wildlife-photography tours led by
founder Rajesh Panwar, with his wife Sheela Panwar, and two birding lodges of their own. The site must advertise all
three lines of business with **equal weight** (user decision):

1. **Group departures** — the 2026–27 calendar of small-group tours across India and abroad, each with its own page,
   seats and (when supplied) price, and a one-tap WhatsApp enquiry.
2. **Custom / private tours** — itineraries designed on request for 50+ Indian destinations and a growing list of
   countries.
3. **Lodges** — Milieu Villa Birding Lodge (their home at Kaladhungi) and Manila Birding Lodge (Almora district).

Success = more qualified WhatsApp/phone/email enquiries per departure and per lodge, fewer "how much is the PNG
tour?"-type questions left unanswered (a real comment on the old site), and a site Rajesh can keep current himself.

## Positioning

The trip is led by the person who is, by his own public eBird record, one of India's most prolific field birders:
top eBirder in India in 2019 (791 species) and 2021 (807), all-time #1 eBirder in Uttarakhand (621 species) and
10th all-time in India (1,155), 1,984 species across 10 countries in 2025 alone. He scouts his own destinations
(Manila, Gangotri/Harsil, Darma & Vyas valleys), lives on the edge of the Kaladhungi reserve forest, runs small groups
"so that everyone gets good chance and space for photography", and publishes an honest trip report with the
species count — including misses — after almost every tour. Neighbouring operators can copy the itinerary, not the
leader, the home patch, or the record.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static marketing site; no payments or accounts. Enquiries go out as pre-filled WhatsApp messages, `tel:` and
  `mailto:` links; any form composes a message rather than posting to a backend.
- **Seats:** shown publicly (user confirmed Rajesh will keep them updated). Current values are the poster's
  "as on 16 Feb 2026" numbers and must be refreshed by Rajesh before launch.
- **Prices:** shown publicly once Rajesh supplies them (user confirmed). None are known today; until then each tour
  reads "itinerary & cost on WhatsApp". Never invent a price.
- Completed 2026 departures stay on the site as trip reports; upcoming ones sort by date.
- Terminology: "departure"/"tour" for fixed-date groups, "custom tour" for private itineraries, "lodge" for the two
  properties, "trip report", "lifer", "life list", "target species", "eBird".

- First belief a new visitor must form: **Rajesh gets you the birds** — the leader's field record is the umbrella that
  departures, custom tours and lodges hang from (user decision).

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over praise.** Every claim is a number, a place, a date or a photograph that already exists in his record.
2. **The leader is the product.** Rajesh and Sheela are visible everywhere — who leads, who hosts, who answers.
3. **Three doors, one conversation.** Departures, custom tours and lodges are equal entry points that all end in the
   same easy WhatsApp conversation.
4. **Seasonal truth.** Show what is actually coming next and what just happened; nothing stale presented as current.
5. **Maintainable by the family.** Updating seats, prices, dates or a trip report is a data edit, not a redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Many guests are older (testimonials mention age explicitly), so generous type sizes, strong contrast
over photography, large tap targets, no hover-only information, and reduced-motion support. Fast on Indian mobile
networks: responsive, compressed imagery; nothing essential behind heavy scripts.
