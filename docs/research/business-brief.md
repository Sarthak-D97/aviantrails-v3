## Origin story (Rajesh's own words, IG 6 May 2026 — paraphrase only)

2008: a Sony bridge camera, a rural-tourism workshop at Jim's Jungle Retreat (Dhela, Corbett). He photographed a male
paradise flycatcher on a guava tree without knowing its name; resource person Imran Khan told him the name and its
breeding behaviour. For five years he focused on promoting rural tourism in Corbett's Village, Chhoti Haldwani.
Switched to birding in **2013**, and realised the Indian Paradise Flycatcher had been his first bird photograph.
A 2016 guest recalls "his life journey from promoting home stay to birder".

## What they sell

1. **Fixed-departure group tours** — birding, bird photography, wildlife photography, nature/astro photography.
   Small groups (Ladakh 2026 had 8 participants). Rajesh leads. Itinerary and cost shared on WhatsApp on request
   (no public prices anywhere). Groups from India plus foreign groups (a Taiwanese photographers' group in Bhutan 2026).
2. **Customised tours** — 50+ destinations across India + international (list below).
3. **Two birding lodges**
   - **Milieu Villa Birding Lodge** — their homestay at Kaladhungi. Summer breeders on the doorstep: Grey-crowned
     Prinia ("probably the best spot in the world to photograph" it), Spot-winged Starling, Crested Bunting,
     Chestnut-winged Cuckoo, Hooded Pitta, Indian Pitta. Pied Thrush ~45 min away. Barati Rau waterfall 14 km.
     Earlier names: Camp Milieu / Milieu Inn (2016–17 reviews: mud huts, feeder, bird bath, home-cooked food).
   - **Manila Birding Lodge** — Manila, Almora district (named for Maa Neela temple), run for ~5 years.
     Cheer Pheasant, Koklass Pheasant, Mountain Scops Owl, Wallcreeper, Upland Pipit.
4. Knowledge: YouTube webinars — "A Decade of Birding", "Chasing Endemic Fauna in Sri Lanka", "Bird Photography in
   Bhutan", "Chasing Rare Birds and Mammals in Ladakh", "Cheer Pheasant", "Discovery of Two Potential Birding
   Destinations in Uttarakhand" (Manila and the Bhagirathi valley).

## Recent trip reports (IG captions — real numbers, safe to quote)

- Ladakh, 10–20 Sep 2026 — 8 participants, 123 bird species, 16 mammals, 2 reptiles. Saker Falcon with a pika kill,
  full-frame Red-fronted Rosefinch, 500+ Tibetan Sandgrouse, Eurasian Eagle-Owl on the ground. Night sky at Pangong Tso.
- Lower Mahananda WS, West Bengal, Aug 2026 — 4 sessions, 75 species; cuckoos & bazas; Pale-headed Woodpecker bonus.
- Colombia, Jul 2026 — 9 antpitta species; Rainbow-bearded Thornbill at eye level; Multicolored Tanager.
- Peru, Jul 2026 — Paradise Tanager (Cock of the Rock Lodge), Grass-green Tanager (Wayqecha).
- Peninsular Malaysia, 7–14 Jun 2026 — 153 species in 8 days; Great Argus, Malayan & Mountain Peacock-Pheasant,
  Rusty-naped and Garnet Pitta; two gibbon species; a pit viper swallowing a squirrel.
- Uttarakhand 10 days, May 2026 — group from Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad; 222 species across 5 zones
  (Kaladhungi, Sattal, Chaukori, Munsyari, Darma Valley, Abbott Mount). Cheer Pheasant, Himalayan Monal, Fire-capped
  Tit, Great Slaty Woodpecker, Bearded Vulture, Golden Eagle…
- Western Arunachal (Eaglenest–Mandala–Sela–Tawang), May 2026 — 256 species; photographed 150+; Gould's Shortwing,
  Sikkim Wedge-billed Babbler, Temminck's Tragopan targets.
- Bhutan, 2–27 Apr 2026 — two back-to-back groups. Taiwanese photographers: 168 species, White-bellied Heron at the
  nest (watched from across the river, no disturbance), Red Panda, Himalayan Monal in snowfall. Indian group: 250+.
- Southern Vietnam, Mar 2026 — 8 days, 177 species + 14 mammals/reptiles, 8 hides; Indian (vegetarian) food at almost
  every stay; add-ons: Ha Long Bay cruise, Angkor Wat equinox.
- Kashmir, Mar 2026 — 20+ Long-eared Owls at one roost.
- Kerala (Thattekad + Munnar), 17–21 Feb 2026 — 139 species. eBird: https://ebird.org/tripreport/478702
- Costa Rica, Jan 2026 — 301 species; first Avian Trails group in five years to see all four Costa Rican monkeys.
- Mongolia, Oct 2025 — a whole day with a male Snow Leopard on a kill; Pallas's Cat; Takhi horses; eagle hunters;
  gers at −12 °C; star trails. Returning Oct 2026.
