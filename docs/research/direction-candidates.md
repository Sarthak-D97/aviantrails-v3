# Direction derivation (new-work §3) — written before the roll

**Mechanism.** Small-group birding and bird-photography tours led in person by one of India's most prolific field
birders, who scouts his own sites, keeps two lodges on his home patches, and reports every trip in species counts.

**Audience scene.** A 58-year-old Pune doctor with a Nikon Z8 and a 180–600, a life list near 900, on his phone at
night between the "Birders of India" WhatsApp group and eBird alerts, deciding between Namdapha in January and
Costa Rica in February. He checks who leads, what the last group actually saw, how many seats are left — then
WhatsApps Rajesh. Also: a Delhi family driving six hours to Kaladhungi for a birding weekend at the lodge.

**Cultural home.** Indian birding: eBird India leaderboards, Grimmett's field guide, BNHS, Nikon culture, WhatsApp
sightings groups; the Kumaon hills and Jim Corbett's village; Indian Railways-style trip planning; Himalayan road trips.

**First surface must prove.** Rajesh gets you the birds — with three equal doors (departures, custom tours, lodges)
and the next departures and seats visible without hunting.

**Rut.** Full-bleed kingfisher hero + "Explore the Birds of India / Book Now", three package cards with "from ₹",
icon row (expert guides · small groups · comfortable stays), testimonial slider, Instagram grid, green-and-earth
palette, feather icon. Predictable opposites: white masonry photographer portfolio; black-and-gold luxury safari.
Literal reading of the name: dotted trail/route maps with a bird icon (one candidate at most — #6).

## Seven systems from the audience's world, ordered by resonance

1. **eBird checklist & leaderboard** (data-graphic / screen) — species tallies, trip-report totals, seasonal
   bar charts, "Top 100 eBirders" tables where he sits at #1. Carries proof-as-data and "we go when the birds are
   there".
2. **Field-guide plate & species account** (print / publishing) — Grimmett-style numbered plates on tinted grounds,
   facing species accounts, range-map thumbnails. Carries target species per tour; the leader as author.
3. **Rajesh's caption line / specimen label** (archival label) — "Species / Place, State / Month Year", his format
   on 1,600 posts; museum data labels. Carries authenticity: every image is a dated record.
4. **Railway reservation chart & departure board** (transport ticketing) — "AVL 02 / RAC / WL / REGRET",
   train-number rows, platform boards. Carries departures and seats in a grammar every Indian reads instantly.
5. **Camera viewfinder & playback overlays** (device interface) — AF box on the eye, shooting-data strip, frame
   counter, histogram. Carries "you'll get the frame" for photographers.
6. **Survey of India topo sheet & BRO pass signage** (cartography / road signage) — contours, spot heights, yellow
   BRO milestones at Sela La and Chele La. Carries scouting and terrain; the literal "trails" reading.
7. **Kumaoni aipan & the Pahadi house** (regional craft / architecture) — white rice-paste line work on geru-red
   grounds, likhai carved doorways. Carries home, hospitality, the lodges and the couple's roots.

Families spanned: 7 (data screen, print, archival label, ticketing, device UI, cartography/signage, folk craft).
